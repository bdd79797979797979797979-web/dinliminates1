## Restaurant contact hardening
- Official chain websites remain mapped directly (including McDonald's).
- Provider contact data is merged instead of stopping at the first Overpass mirror.
- Missing website OR phone now qualifies for contact enrichment.
- Optional Google Places Nearby lookup supplies local restaurant website and phone data when the deployment has GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY or GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY.
- Optional exact-business Google Places text lookup is used for remaining missing contacts.
- When a local phone is still unavailable, the app gives a Google phone-search fallback rather than displaying a dead/blank contact.
- Unknown websites fall back to a Google restaurant-website search.
- Restaurant cards expose Website/Google and Directions actions; real phone numbers remain tappable to call.
- Restaurant Details exposes phone, website/search, and Google Maps directions.

## Important deployment note
Google Places enrichment requires a valid server-side API key in the Netlify/Vercel function environment. Without that key, official-chain website mappings and Google search fallbacks still work, while store-specific phone numbers depend on OSM/Photon/ArcGIS data.
